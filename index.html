Plain-language summary. We use customer messages and the contact or account information made available through authorized Meta services to answer product questions, understand customization requirements, and prepare quotations. We do not sell personal data.

1. Who We Are

This service is operated by JIANGSU SMILE TOOLS CO., LTD. (“Smile,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), an international trade company that supplies custom fragrance products to customers outside China.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies when a potential or existing customer communicates with Smile through our website or through Meta products such as Facebook and Instagram, including when our automated assistant helps answer inquiries, introduce products, collect customization requirements, or prepare a quotation.

3. Information We May Process

The information we process depends on what you send to us and the permissions available through the relevant Meta service. It may include:

  • your name, username, account or page identifier, profile picture, and other basic profile information made available by Meta;
  • messages, comments, inquiry details, and the content of your communications with Smile;
  • business contact details you choose to provide, such as company name, email address, telephone number, country, and shipping destination;
  • product and customization requirements, including product type, fragrance, materials, packaging, quantities, target price, artwork, samples, and delivery requirements;
  • quotation history, order-related communications, and customer service records; and
  • basic technical and security records needed to operate and protect the service.

Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for a product inquiry or quotation.

4. How We Use Information

We process information to:

  • identify the conversation and respond to customer inquiries;
  • automatically introduce suitable products and customization options;
  • prepare indicative or formal quotations based on the requirements provided;
  • follow up on samples, orders, production, shipping, and after-sales service;
  • improve the accuracy and quality of our customer service tools;
  • prevent abuse, protect our systems, and comply with applicable law; and
  • maintain records needed for legitimate business, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes.

Automated responses and quotations may require confirmation by our staff. A message generated by the assistant does not create a binding sales contract unless Smile separately confirms the order and commercial terms.

5. Meta Platform Data

When you contact us through Facebook or Instagram, Meta may provide data to us in accordance with your settings, the permissions granted to our application, and Meta's applicable terms. We use that data only to provide the customer communication and quotation functions described in this policy. You may manage or revoke connected-app permissions through your Facebook or Instagram settings.

6. Sharing and International Processing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only with employees and service providers who need it to operate the inquiry, quotation, hosting, communications, security, or order-fulfilment functions; with professional advisers; or where disclosure is required by law. Because we serve international customers and operate from China, information may be processed in China and in countries where our service providers operate. We apply reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and the service.

7. Retention and Security

We retain inquiry and quotation information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain the business relationship, support an order, meet legal or accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Retention periods may vary according to the nature of the inquiry and whether it results in an order. We use reasonable organizational and technical measures to guard against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also stop communicating with the assistant and revoke the application's access through your Meta account settings.

9. User Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of data held by Smile, contact us using the telephone number or website listed below. Please state that your request concerns Smile user data deletion and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant conversation, such as your Facebook or Instagram username and the approximate date of contact. Do not send your password.

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize data that we are not required to retain and confirm completion through an available contact channel. You can also remove the application's Meta access through Facebook or Instagram settings; this prevents future access but may not automatically delete records already retained by Smile.

10. Children

Our business-to-business product inquiry service is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this service.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.
